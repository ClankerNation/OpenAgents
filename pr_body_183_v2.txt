Rework for #183 — Gas sponsorship relay with executeOnBehalf

  for gas-sponsored meta-transactions

- Nonce tracking per agent prevents replay attacks
- Relayer pays gas, reimbursed from agent's staked balance
- SponsoredExecution and StakeDeposited events
- TaskRouter is Ownable for secure ownership

Tests: sponsored execution, replay prevention (nonce), insufficient stake,
invalid signature.
